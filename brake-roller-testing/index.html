Why Brake Roller Testing Matters

Brakes are the most critical safety system on any vehicle. Brake fade, uneven pad wear, and axle imbalance can all reduce stopping power without obvious warning signs. Our professional brake roller test measures actual stopping force at each wheel, identifying problems before they become dangerous or costly.

  • Accurate stopping distance measurement at each wheel
  • Identifies brake imbalance and axle issues
  • Helps pass roadworthiness inspections
  • Trusted by fleets, tradies, and local drivers
  • Suitable for cars, 4WDs, light trucks and trailers

Our fully certified test equipment produces a printed report showing braking performance across all axles. If results reveal a problem, we can advise on repairs or carry them out the same day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need this test for a roadworthy certificate?
Brake performance is assessed as part of every roadworthy inspection. Our brake roller test gives a quantified, documented result that satisfies the VIC Roads roadworthy standard.
How long does brake roller testing take?
The test itself typically takes 15 to 30 minutes. Including paperwork and a walkthrough of results, allow around 45 minutes for your visit.
What vehicles can be tested?
We test most passenger cars, 4WDs, utes, light trucks and trailers. Contact us before booking if you have a particularly heavy or oversized vehicle.
What happens if my brakes fail the test?
We'll provide a clear explanation of which axle or system underperformed. In many cases we can carry out the required brake repairs the same day to get you back on the road.
