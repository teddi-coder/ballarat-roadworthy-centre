Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ballarat Roadworthy Centre ABN [TBC] ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, holds and discloses personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. What Personal Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name, email address and phone number (provided via contact or booking forms)
  • Vehicle make, model and year
  • Service requirements and related information you provide
  • Location data (if you use map features)
  • Website usage data collected through cookies and analytics tools (including Google Tag Manager)

2. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information directly from you when you:

  • Submit a booking or enquiry form on our website
  • Contact us by phone, SMS or email
  • Visit our premises

We may also collect information automatically through website analytics tools.

3. Why We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information to:

  • Respond to your booking enquiries and service requests
  • Provide automotive workshop services
  • Contact you regarding your vehicle appointment
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Our staff involved in providing your requested services
  • Third-party service providers (e.g. Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager) under confidentiality obligations
  • Government bodies where required by law

5. Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. Personal information is stored securely, and access is restricted to authorised personnel.

6. Access and Correction

You have the right to access and request correction of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at contact@ballaratroadworthy.com.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and Google Tag Manager to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function correctly.

8. Complaints

If you have a complaint about how we handle your personal information, please contact us at contact@ballaratroadworthy.com. If we cannot resolve the matter, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.
