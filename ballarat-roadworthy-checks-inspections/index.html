Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

You need an RWC when selling a registered vehicle, transferring registration, or re-registering a vehicle that has been unregistered. It is not required for routine annual rego renewals.
Most inspections are completed within 45–90 minutes. We’ll give you an accurate estimate when you book. Same-day appointments are often available.
If your vehicle fails, we’ll explain exactly what needs to be rectified and give you a written list. You can choose to have repairs done with us or elsewhere — there’s no pressure.
Bookings are recommended to minimise wait times. However, if you’re in a pinch, call us and we’ll do our best to fit you in on the day.
