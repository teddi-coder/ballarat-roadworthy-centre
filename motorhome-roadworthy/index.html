Why Motorhome Roadworthy Inspections Matter

A roadworthy certificate is required when selling or re-registering your motorhome, transferring ownership, or returning a vehicle to the road after extended storage. Unlike standard passenger cars, motorhomes present unique inspection challenges due to their size, weight, and dual-purpose nature as both a vehicle and a living space.

At Ballarat Roadworthy Centre, our licensed inspectors are experienced with campervans, motorhomes and heavy RVs of all configurations. We check every safety-critical system to ensure your vehicle is legal, safe, and ready for the road ahead.

What Happens During Your Motorhome Roadworthy Inspection

  • Brakes — stopping performance, pad depth, balance and hose condition
  • Tyres — tread depth, sidewall condition, correct rating for vehicle weight
  • Steering and suspension — play, wear, component integrity
  • Lights — all external lighting, indicators, brake lights and reversing lights
  • Structural integrity — chassis, body panels, floor and door mechanisms
  • Windscreen and glazing — chips, cracks, wiper condition
  • Engine and exhaust — leaks, emissions, mounting condition

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an RWC for my motorhome to renew rego?
In Victoria, a roadworthy certificate is required when transferring ownership of a vehicle, not for standard annual registration renewal. However, if your registration has lapsed or you're bringing the vehicle back from unregistered status, an RWC may be required. Our team can advise you based on your specific situation.
How long does a motorhome inspection take?
Most motorhome roadworthy inspections take between 45 minutes and 90 minutes depending on the size and condition of the vehicle. We'll give you a more accurate timeframe when you book.
What does the inspection cover?
We check all safety-critical systems including brakes, tyres, steering, suspension, lights, glazing, structural integrity, and the engine and exhaust system. The inspection follows VIC Roads standards for roadworthy certification.
Can you inspect large or heavy RVs?
Yes. As an NHVR-approved inspection facility, we are equipped to inspect large motorhomes, heavy RVs, and long-wheelbase campervans. Contact us to discuss your specific vehicle before booking.
