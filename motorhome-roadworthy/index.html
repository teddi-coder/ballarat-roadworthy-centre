Why Motorhome Roadworthy Inspections Matter

A Roadworthy Certificate is required when selling or transferring ownership of your motorhome, re-registering a vehicle after a period off the road, returning a long-stored motorhome back to use, or applying for an unregistered vehicle permit. Without a valid RWC you cannot legally transfer registration, and if there are safety issues you are putting yourself and others at risk.

Whether you are preparing your campervan for sale, re-registering after storage, or bringing a newly purchased RV onto Victorian roads, our team will carry out a thorough inspection and let you know exactly where you stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an RWC for my motorhome to renew rego?
You need a Roadworthy Certificate when transferring registration, selling the vehicle, or bringing an unregistered motorhome back onto the road. Standard rego renewal does not always require an RWC, but your motorhome must still be in a roadworthy condition at all times.
How long does a motorhome inspection take?
Most motorhome inspections are completed within 1 to 2 hours, depending on the size and condition of the vehicle. We will give you an accurate timeframe when you book.
What does the inspection cover?
We check brakes, tyres, steering, suspension, lights, windscreen, body structure, seatbelts, and other safety-critical components as required under VIC Roads standards.
Can you inspect large or heavy RVs?
Yes. Our facility is NHVR-approved and set up to handle larger motorhomes and RVs, including those that exceed standard passenger vehicle dimensions. Call us to confirm your vehicle size.
