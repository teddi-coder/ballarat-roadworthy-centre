Why Motorhome Roadworthy Inspections Matter

A roadworthy certificate is required when selling a motorhome, re-registering after a period off the road, transferring registration, or returning a vehicle to Victorian roads. Without a valid RWC, you can face fines, registration refusal, and serious liability if an accident occurs.

At Ballarat Roadworthy Centre, we make the process straightforward. Our licensed inspectors know exactly what VicRoads requires for motorhomes of every size, and we give you a clear pass or fail result with guidance on any rectifications needed.
